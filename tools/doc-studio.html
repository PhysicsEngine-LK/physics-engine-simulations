How it works

Build the document from blocks (left panel). Pages are filled automatically — when a page is full, the next block moves to a new page. Questions are numbered automatically with purple badges. Answer blocks only appear in the Teacher PDF; in the Student PDF they are removed (or replaced with blank lines — see settings / ⚙ on the answer).

To make the PDF: click Teacher PDF or Student PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the print window. Simulation buttons stay clickable in the PDF, and sim cards also get a QR code for printed copies.

Hover over a block to see its tools on the left: ↑ ↓ move, ⚙ options (numbering, show-in Teacher/Student only, blank lines, MCQ columns…), ⧉ duplicate, 🗑 delete.
