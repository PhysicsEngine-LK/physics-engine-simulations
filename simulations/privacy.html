Privacy Policy

Physics Engine (A/L Physics, Sri Lanka) · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy covers Physics Engine Studio, a private tool that Physics Engine uses to publish its own posts (lesson posters and short videos) to its own Facebook Page and Telegram channel. It also covers this website.

What the app does

Physics Engine Studio runs only on the Physics Engine owner's own computer. It is used only by the Physics Engine owner, to post content to the Physics Engine Facebook Page when the owner clicks “Post”. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can log in to it.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal information about students, visitors or other Facebook users. It does not read messages, comments, followers or friend lists.

To post to the Page, the app keeps one Facebook Page access token. That token is stored only on the owner's computer and is sent only to Facebook. It is never sold, shared or sent anywhere else.

How information is used

The Page access token is used only to publish posts that the owner chooses, to the Physics Engine Facebook Page.

Data deletion

The app holds no data about anyone except the Page owner's own access token. To remove it:

  • The owner can click “Disconnect” in the app, which deletes the saved token, or remove the app in Facebook under Settings → Security and login → Business integrations.
  • Anyone who believes the app has data about them can email physicsengine.official@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. We will reply within 7 days and delete anything found.
