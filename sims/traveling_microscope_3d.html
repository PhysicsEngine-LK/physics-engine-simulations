Setup

Place your model file at:

sims/assets/traveling_microscope.glb

If you use another filename, update the MODEL_URL value inside this page.

This page must be opened through http://localhost or another local server. Opening it as file:// will not load the model.

Student controls:

  • Drag to orbit around the microscope
  • Scroll to zoom in or out
  • Right-drag to pan the view
